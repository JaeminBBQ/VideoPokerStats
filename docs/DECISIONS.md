# Decisions (append-only)

**D1 (2026-10-03): Exact engine via subset sums + inclusion–exclusion.** One table build per game (~1 s) makes every hand's 32 hold EVs exact and nearly free, and lets the verifier play all 2.6M starting hands in seconds with the *same code* the app uses. Rejected: per-hand brute-force enumeration (2.6M evaluations/hand; fine for one hand, too slow to verify), sampling (not exact).

**D2 (2026-10-03): First game is NSUD Deuces Wild (GSR), not Full-Pay Deuces Wild.** Research: FPDW has had no known US location since Sam's Town pulled it in early 2023. 10/7 DB exists only at Vegas Station Casinos. Reno-Tahoe has no game ≥ 100%. Owner plays GSR and Legends Bay, so the trainer starts with the best game they can actually play (NSUD 99.73%) plus Legends Bay's Illinois Deuces (98.91%). All seven games are in the engine and verified; UI defaults to NSUD.

**D3 (2026-10-03): Loose Deuces = the 5K-pays-15 table (100.97%).** The 101.60% version (5K pays 17) was removed from the D in 2015. The engine computed 100.9695% for 800/500/25/15/10/4/3/2/2/1, matching Wizard of Odds exactly.

**D4 (2026-10-03): Client-only app, stats in localStorage.** Single user, no secrets, no server needed; the engine runs in a Web Worker. Revisit if the owner wants cross-device sync (phone at the casino vs desktop).

**D5 (2026-10-03): Charts are generated, never typed.** A hold-pattern classifier names every hold (e.g. "2 deuces + 3 to a royal"); the chart is the pattern order that best reproduces perfect play over all starting hands, and the generator reports its error vs perfect play.

**D6 (2026-10-03): Chart lines are ordered by direct EV maximization, not pairwise votes.** The first draft (greedy pairwise ordering) gave up 2.15%; local search on the chart's total EV, plus deuce-aware straight "ways" and A/T qualifiers on small royal draws, brought NSUD to 0.014% given up. The chart is also the drill-mode vocabulary: "similar hands" = same (correct line, chosen line) confusion.

**D7 (2026-10-04): Drill hands don't count toward per-game totals.** Drills are deliberately the user's weak spots, so mixing them in would make the error rate measure the drill mix instead of real play. Drill accuracy is shown separately. A confusion clears after 3 correct drills in a row. Games without a chart classifier drill the same misplayed hand with suits/order disguised. Resetting a game's stats clears its history too (accepted from T001).

**D8 (2026-10-04): Paytables photographed on real machines become games once the engine verifies them.** Legends Bay Game King #12139 at 10¢: JoB 8/5, Bonus 6/5, BPD 7/5, and Deuces 16/13. All four computed returns match Wizard of Odds (the deuces table to 6 digits, 0.967651), which also confirms the photos were read correctly. vpFREE2's Legends Bay listing ($1+ multi-game, Illinois deuces 98.91%) is a different denomination/machine, so `illinois-deuces` stays as listed. These are all below 97.3%; GSR NSUD (99.73%) remains the owner's best local game.

**D9 (2026-10-04): Jacks or Better family gets its own classifier; casino tabs replace the dropdown.** Owner wants to practice JoB 8/5 (best photographed Legends Bay game). One natural-card classifier (`naturalPattern`) covers JoB/Bonus/BPD/DB/DDB; the JoB chart gives up 0.002%. Venue is engine data (`GameDef.venue`) so tabs need no hard-coded lists. Claude made the small UI wording changes itself (chart rule/titles from JSON, drill prompt section labels) so that commit stayed consistent; DeepSeek was idle.

**D10 (2026-10-04): Only photographed paytables ship. Supersedes D2, D3, and the game lists in D8/D9.** Owner: "remove Vegas, Reference and the GSR stats or any stats we don't have proof for." Removed NSUD (GSR, vpFREE2 only), Illinois deuces (vpFREE2 only), Full-Pay and Loose Deuces, 10/7 DB, 10/6 DDB, Joker Poker, their charts, and the Joker evaluator. Kept the four games photographed on Legends Bay #12139 at 10¢; each `GameDef` carries its `proof` photo. Default game: Jacks or Better 8/5; default denomination 10¢ (the photographed machine). The engine stays general (deuces + Jacks-or-Better classifiers; card encoding still supports a joker), so a newly photographed paytable is a data entry plus `npm run verify` and `npm run chart`. Old localStorage records for removed games are ignored, not deleted.

**D11 (2026-10-04): Deal mode gets a bankroll session with real draws; tier points = 1 per $1 coin-in.** Owner: choose a starting amount and a denomination (5¢/10¢/25¢, default 5¢, always max bet), and earn 1 tier point per dollar wagered. After grading, the machine's draw replaces the unheld cards from the 47 undealt cards (`draw` in `src/engine/cards.ts`), and the final hand is paid from the paytable at 5 coins × denomination. Money is integer cents (`src/lib/bankroll.ts`). Points count coin-in, not losses. Drill hands don't touch the bankroll (same reason as D7). One session bankroll carries across games. The bar also shows expected loss at perfect play (coin-in × (1 − return)). $1 dropped from the selector. **Caveat:** the four paytables were photographed at 10¢. Machines often use different paytables at other denominations, so 5¢/25¢ play here assumes the same table until the owner photographs those denominations. Supersedes D10's 10¢ default.

**D12 (2026-10-04): All common denominations, 1¢ to $5. Amends D11's 5¢/10¢/25¢ list.** Owner: "Add the other denominations... I know for a fact it didn't change up to 25 cents." The selector offers 1¢, 5¢, 10¢, 25¢, 50¢, $1, $2, $5, and every denomination plays the four photographed paytables. `CONFIRMED_DENOMINATIONS` (5¢/10¢/25¢, the owner's word) marks the rest with an asterisk and a note until the owner checks those machines. A different paytable at a denomination would become its own photographed, verified game (D10), not a silent swap.

**D13 (2026-10-05): Tier points are 1 per $2 of coin-in, not $1. Amends D11.** Owner corrected the casino's rate. `CENTS_PER_POINT = 200`. At 5¢ max bet ($0.25/hand) that's 8 hands per point. Lifetime and session points are computed from coin-in, so they recompute at the new rate. Already-logged sessions keep the `points` value stored when they ended.

**D14 (2026-10-05): Bankroll-survival numbers are exact, and a simulation cross-checks them.** Owner asked how much bankroll each bet size needs at 0–1% error. Exact per-hand outcome distributions (`src/engine/outcomes.ts`, full enumeration, perfect play reproduces all 4 published returns) feed an exact risk-of-ruin DP (`src/lib/risk.ts`): ruin = can't cover a max bet, mistake = next-best hold on that hand with probability e. Output: `docs/bankroll/RISK.md` + `risk.json`. An independent engine simulation (`scripts/bankroll-sim.ts`, 240M hands) agrees at 20 checkpoints (|z| ≤ 2.1). Finding: at 0–2% error the bankroll needed moves only ~1–4%. Swings dominate. Mistakes show up in expected loss instead.

**D15 (2026-10-07): Assist tab = manual hand entry, not camera detection.** Owner wants to know the correct hold on their home machine. They looked at iPhone camera detection (and Even Realities glasses as a display) and chose a thumb-friendly entry page instead: 5 ranks, then 5 suits, and the engine shows the hold, ties included. Assist hands are not recorded to stats, drills, or the bankroll, because they aren't the owner's own decisions. Camera detection is shelved. Note for the record: Nevada law (NRS 465.075) prohibits using a device to analyze strategy at a licensed gaming establishment, so this tab is for home play and post-session review, not for use at a casino machine.

**D16 (2026-10-08): Paytables are tied to denominations, and max bet is per machine. GSR is the second venue.** The owner photographed GSR: machine #13013 runs 5¢–$1 with a different paytable at each denomination (5¢ JoB 9/5 vs $1 JoB 9/6), and max bet is 20 coins at 5¢ and 10 coins at $1, not 5. Each `GameDef` now carries `offers: { denomination, maxCoins, confirmed }[]`. A photographed (game, denomination) whose paytable differs is its own game, so the GSR names include the denomination. Bets, payouts, tier coin-in, the trainer's "cost at" line, and bankroll dollars all use `maxCoinsAt(game, denomination)`. Changing games snaps the denomination to one the game is offered at. The compare table lists only games offered at the chosen denomination. Legends Bay keeps every denomination at 5 coins, with 5¢–25¢ confirmed (D11, D12). New games: GSR JoB 9/5 (98.4498%), JoB 9/6 (99.5439%), Double Bonus 9/7/5 (99.1065%), Deuces Wild Bonus Poker 9/4/4 (99.4502%), Deuces Wild 20/12/10 (0.975791). All five match Wizard of Odds or videopokertrainer.org. The deuces classifier now names aces and five-of-a-kind rank groups in bonus-deuces games, so the DWBP chart gives up 0.004% (Wizard's exceptionless strategy: 99.4464%; ours: 99.4465%). The natural classifier separates aces full where four aces pay a bonus. Progressive royals (25¢ and up) are computed at the 800 reset. Claude made the UI wiring itself because the money math had to change in the same commit. DeepSeek was idle.
- *D16 addendum (same day, after validation):* `settings.denomination` is now the player's **preferred** denomination, and each game plays `snapDenomination(game, preferred)` without overwriting it. Before, a trip to a $1-only GSR game and back silently left a 5¢ session betting at $1. When the balance can't cover the current game's bet, the trainer now says so and offers "End session", where it used to show a misleading "Out of credits / New session". The session log records every denomination a session played (`denominations`; older entries keep `denomination`). An independent validator confirmed all five paytables against the photos and Wizard of Odds.
